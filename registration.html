Week 2: Festive Fiesta

June 29th - July 3rd

Get ready for the most wonderful time of the year! From spooky Halloween fun, to Easter hunts and even Jolly Holidays; campers will dive into a world of festive fun. There will be themed games and seasonal surprises all week long, culminating in a Red, White and Blue celebration and cookout to end the week. Campers will be sure to make memories that are merry, bright and unforgettable.

Week 3: Goals and Games

July 6th - July 10th

This week is an action packed week full of hands-on sports and collaborative games, as well as board games, card games and more. To add to the challenge and fun, campers will participate in a week long event - The Camp Connor Olympics. Campers will earn points for their team, participate in a "color run" and on Friday we have one final camp-wide competition and celebrate all our team accomplishments.

This week includes a camp-wide field trip for even more animal adventure!

Campership Program

Thanks to the generous contributions of some Poland residents and businesses, we are continuing our scholarship program for Poland children to attend Camp Connor this summer at a significantly reduced rate. Any Poland resident going into 1st thru 8th grade can apply if they are eligible for “free” lunch through their school.

Note: Poland home school families can apply as well, with the understanding that financial information would need to be provided to verify need. Please contact our office if you have questions @ 998-4650. Campership will be awarded to families starting March 1st. We will consider a maximum campership of $140.00 off per week of camp, for up to 6 weeks. The overall community need and available funding will determine how many weeks we can actually provide.
