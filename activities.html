Activities

Swimming

Each day at Camp, campers will have an opportunity to swim with their group down at our waterfront on Lower Range Pond. Our staff and lifeguards are present at all times.

Art

From themed crafts and music to dancing, theatrical performances and more! Campers will enjoy being creative, making beautiful works of art and melodious music too!

Sports and Games

A variety of different sports and games will be offered each day. From basketball to kickball to 4-square and more! Campers will have access to our field, obstacle course and basketball court too!

Outdoor Life

With 12 acres of beautiful Maine woods to explore, campers will have fun hiking, learning about our trees and animals, and more.

Kayaking

Groups of all ages will have opportunities throughout the summer to go boating. Campers who have boating experience and can show paddling ability may choose to use a single kayak. Inexperienced boaters or campers who are not comfortable boating on their own will be paired with a more experienced boater.

Fishing

They will need parental consent in order to participate. Campers who are attending fishing trips may bring their own poles, tackle, and or bait, to camp with them on trip days. Fishing gear will be labeled with campers' names and stored safely in the staff wing before and after use.
